How to use the KakoBuy spreadsheet

  1. 1. Check the listing

    Open the HipobuyFind product page. Check the source seller, current price in yuan and available variants. Compare the seller’s size chart with a garment or pair of shoes you already own.

  2. 2. Estimate the delivered cost

    Add domestic shipping, agent and payment fees, and international shipping to the item price. Include any applicable taxes and duties. Compare courier quotes using both parcel weight and dimensions.

  3. 3. Review warehouse photos

    If your agent provides quality-control photos, check the size label, color and measurements against your order. Resolve any discrepancies before approving international dispatch.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Who provides the listings on this page?

This independent guide uses HipobuyFind listings. The main buttons open HipobuyFind, and each product name opens its corresponding listing.

Can I use a listing with KakoBuy?

Open the HipobuyFind listing and look for the original seller link. Paste that seller URL into KakoBuy and confirm that it resolves to the correct product with the variant you want before placing an order.

When should I compare shipping quotes?

Get a rough estimate before buying. Once your agent has the packed weight and dimensions, compare available shipping lines and check the final quote before dispatch.
